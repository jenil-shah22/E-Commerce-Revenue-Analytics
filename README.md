## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a large-scale ecommerce marketplace dataset to uncover patterns in customer behavior, seller performance, product demand, delivery efficiency, and revenue growth.

Using Python-based analytics and machine learning techniques, the project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and forecasting models.

---

## Business Context

Modern ecommerce platforms generate millions of records across customers, orders, payments, reviews, products, and logistics operations.

Business leaders often need answers to questions such as:

- Which factors influence customer ratings?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- How dependent is the platform on top-performing sellers?
- Which regions generate the highest business value?
- What revenue trends can be expected in the future?

This project addresses these questions through descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive analytics.

# Customer Analytics

## Objective

Understand the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

### Analysis Performed

- Review Score Analysis
- Delivery Performance Comparison
- Customer Experience Correlation Study

### Major Findings

- Faster deliveries tend to receive better customer ratings.
- Delayed shipments significantly impact customer satisfaction.
- Delivery efficiency is strongly associated with review performance.

---

# Product Analytics

## Objective

Identify categories driving marketplace performance.

### Analysis Performed

- Revenue by Category
- Sales Volume Comparison
- Premium Product Category Identification

### Major Findings

- Revenue generation varies significantly between categories.
- Some low-volume categories generate disproportionately high revenue.
- Category-specific strategies can improve profitability.

---

# Seller Analytics

## Objective

Evaluate seller contribution across the marketplace.

### Analysis Performed

- Seller Revenue Ranking
- Contribution Distribution
- Seller Concentration Analysis

### Major Findings

- A relatively small group of sellers contributes a large share of total revenue.
- High-performing sellers play a critical role in marketplace growth.
- Seller segmentation can support partnership decisions.

---

# Geographic Analytics

## Objective

Analyze regional demand and revenue patterns.

### Analysis Performed

- Revenue by State
- Order Distribution
- Revenue per Order

### Major Findings

- Revenue is concentrated in a limited number of states.
- Customer purchasing behavior differs across regions.
- Untapped regions present potential expansion opportunities.

---

# Revenue Forecasting

## Objective

Estimate future marketplace revenue using historical trends.

### Model Used

- Linear Regression

### Forecasting Process

- Monthly Revenue Aggregation
- Time-Based Feature Engineering
- Model Training
- Revenue Prediction

### Results

- Historical growth trends indicate continued revenue expansion.
- Forecasts provide a baseline for planning and budgeting decisions.
- The model can be enhanced using advanced time-series techniques.
